- The human created Jujutsu checkpoints at several review boundaries.
- During one canonical-semantic-ownership follow-up, the agent had difficulty obtaining some mechanical counts from scripts. The human assisted with obtaining those counts.
- The human supplied external review/correction instructions between several agent runs.
- Near final Milestone 8 completion, the agent stopped without writing the requested final review file. The human explicitly prompted it to write that file; the resulting report may therefore have had less conversational context than the preceding implementation run.
- Agent/subagent self-review is not treated as an external review verdict.
- The final external completion decision was made only after the post-archive follow-up evidence was reviewed.

These interventions are evaluation metadata. They must not be supplied to an agent being evaluated on the original semantic-reconciliation task.

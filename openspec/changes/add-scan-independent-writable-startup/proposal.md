## Why

Current canonical requirements already own identity and topology acceptance, store and recovery writer claims, conservative recovery reopen, operation-slot lifetime, range-basis vocabulary, checksum-owner evidence, capability-profile evidence, and frontend publication. Durable writable-session begin exists only in the separately reviewed active `define-portable-writable-session-lifecycle` target; it remains external, unsynced, and non-canonical in this lineage. The missing startup fact is bounded physical quiescence across a normal custody gap: after the exact current writer claim or exclusion and stable backing-resource/alias binding are acquired, no covered pre-boundary effect within the selected capability/profile's declared and evidence-supported physical reachability universe may mutate that resource after a defined boundary. The file-backed/store ownership boundary allocates and revalidates the claim/resource binding; the store-operation boundary is the sole canonical producer because it establishes exact predecessor physical reachability/closure within that universe while the binding remains held.

Without this fact, a complete unambiguous array can be forced through a payload-sized scan or published while a covered pre-boundary effect can still arrive late. This change makes the already-proposed typed store-scoped `AdmissionStabilizationEvidence` the one canonical producer of that cross-custody physical no-late fact. Startup consumes one successful, exactly current-bound result per required store whose scope covers every route/effect within the selected profile's declared physical reachability universe that is still capable of reaching the same stable backing resource, including unattributed old kernel/device queues, DMA, raw or out-of-band paths, aliases, and remaps when that profile includes them. Routes and actors explicitly outside the selected profile remain explicit assumptions and non-claims; startup does not silently broaden the profile, invent a second producer, historical summary, or persistent operation ledger. Current canonical requirements remain authoritative until this active change is reviewed, implemented, verified, and synced.

## What Changes

- Make `store-operation-contracts` the sole canonical producer of typed `AdmissionStabilizationEvidence`, with the file-backed/store ownership owner as the sole allocator and revalidator of the opaque exact current claim token and stable-resource/alias binding. Before stabilization, that owner acquires and then holds both continuously through every store-evidence premise boundary. Success means only that every covered pre-boundary effect in the complete predecessor physical-reachability/closure scope within the selected capability/profile's declared physical reachability universe has completed or ceased, so none can mutate after the boundary. This is quiescence, not a persistence fence: already-visible bytes gain no stronger durability evidence. The result binds current claim/incarnation/domain, stable resource/alias identity, selected capability/profile and declared universe, exact physical scope, assignment/topology, monotonic boundary, and opaque provenance. Writable-recovery authority is separately acquired and held/freshly revalidated alongside the store claims through stabilization, each pre-commit owner gate, epoch commit, and observation of its result; healthy service and operator start independently continue authority holding/revalidation from that observation through admission return, handoff, and publication.
- Keep current in-process children, resources, completions, and reconciliation under `OperationSlotTable` and its related owners. Store stabilization respects effects within the selected profile's declared universe that remain capable of reaching the stable backing resource, including predecessor domains, but does not derive operation outcome, terminality, release, or transaction meaning and does not require a persistent per-operation history across reboot.
- Add durable protection-epoch admission to `recovery-state-semantics`. The recovery-state owner itself produces and durably preserves a range-role basis snapshot from accepted current recovery inspection, bound to exact recovery generation, topology epoch, role, range, and source-owner evidence. Its canonical values remain `current`, `prior`, `unprotected`, `indeterminate`, and `not-yet-interpretable`; absent, stale, conflicting, insufficient, or mismatched source evidence refuses admission. The transition consumes successful, freshly revalidated exact per-store stabilization evidence and checksum-owner evidence compared under exact target/range, topology epoch, digest profile, checksum-set generation, target content generation, and persistence-evidence bindings. It preserves separate coded-range-authority and recovery `CLEAN` owner observations without combining, relabeling, reconstructing, turning either into a startup input, or claiming `CLEAN`; it consumes durable session-begin evidence only from the reviewed active lifecycle target. Recovery holds and freshly revalidates the exact store and writable-recovery authorities through the epoch commit and observation of its result, and does not certify authority continuity through later admission return, frontend handoff, or publication.
- Add the portable orchestration to `healthy-portable-io`: reacquire authority through the file-backed/store owner; acquire and continue holding each exact current claim token and stable-resource/alias binding and writable-recovery authority; obtain store stabilization covering every route/effect within the selected profile's declared physical reachability universe that remains capable of reaching the resource; consume exact checksum evidence, the recovery-produced range-role basis snapshot, and separate coded-range-authority and recovery `CLEAN` observations; obtain durable session begin from the reviewed lifecycle target; freshly revalidate all authority and binding after each pre-commit gate and through the epoch commit observation; then independently continue holding and revalidating those authorities from the observed commit through admission return, frontend handoff, and successful publication before returning or publishing.
- Modify operator start composition so admission and actual publication remain distinct. A durable admission can survive frontend refusal or failure; start independently keeps the exact authorities and bindings current from the observed epoch commit through admission return, handoff, and successful publication, while preserving its session/epoch evidence and owner next action without rollback, automatic close, automatic re-begin, or a stronger public claim when later authority or publication fails.
- Preserve coded-range-authority observation and recovery `CLEAN` capture/state/uncertainty independently from operation release. Startup consumes each exact owner observation as a separately named prerequisite; it does not combine or relabel them, derive a startup-owned input or claim, reconstruct coded membership, capture frontier, or future exclusion, or claim `CLEAN`. It preserves each owner observation and consequence across the epoch and blocks only according to that owner’s canonical refusal or reconciliation result.
- Define conservative refusal and restart behavior for ambiguous identity, unavailable stores, known or uncertain partial claim acquisition, claim/resource/alias or writable-recovery-authority loss or remap at any gate, missing or mismatched stabilization, unsupported/failed/uncertain stabilization, incomplete physical predecessor reachability coverage, a new incarnation with unclosed predecessor effects, checksum evidence absent or mismatched in exact target/range, topology epoch, digest profile, checksum-set generation, target content generation, or persistence evidence, invalid recovery basis source evidence or bindings, unresolved coded-range-authority observation, unresolved recovery `CLEAN` capture/state, rejected or uncertain session/epoch commits, and unavailable independently owned publication gates. Known partial multi-store acquisition continues to consume the file-backed owner’s current definite release-all rule for every acquired partial lease. After the complete required file-backed set has been accepted, an abandoned startup attempt requests the exact `FileBackedReleaseObservation` defined by `req.file-backed-stores.post-acquisition-writer-claim-release-is-exact-and-owner-observed`: `released-all` establishes only absence of live ownership for that old set and permits only a later fresh acquisition under normal revalidation, while `residual` or `uncertain` remains reconciliation-required and cannot authorize inferred release, same-process reacquisition, or evidence reuse. Process loss uses the existing crash-release rule and a later process reacquires fresh. Writable-recovery authority follows its separate owner disposition, and file-backed cleanup does not roll back durable session/epoch or other owner state.
- Bound success to a complete stable assignment set, the selected profile's bounded availability observations, and its declared and evidence-supported physical reachability universe. Admission does not claim routes or actors explicitly outside that universe and does not prove operation outcome, persistence or durability, payload readability or correctness, parity/checksum validity, `CLEAN`, custody continuity, range basis, `Current`, current protection, release, or full recoverability.

## Dependencies and Evidence

The target composes current operation-slot lifetime/reclaim and store ownership, the separately recovered reviewed writable-session candidate from `define-portable-writable-session-lifecycle` (including `req.recovery-state-semantics.durable-writable-session-lifecycle-binds-authority-and-close-evidence`), which remains an active external prerequisite until integrated, reviewed, and canonicalized, plus two separate external coded-range-authority and recovery `CLEAN` prerequisites from `define-coded-range-clean-coordination`: `req.explicit-transaction-machine.coded-range-authority-covers-shared-parity-conflicts` and `req.dirty-integrity-invalidation.recovery-clean-captures-a-closed-mutation-set`. The file-backed-stores owner currently requires release of every partial lease. The active `define-file-backed-post-acquisition-release` target adds the distinct exact release observation needed only after an accepted complete claim set is later relinquished; it remains a proposed prerequisite until reviewed and canonicalized. Startup consumes that owner result but retains ownership of when an attempt is abandoned and release is requested. All four target/prerequisite areas must complete their normal authority transitions before startup implementation; this change remains proposed and unsynced.

The store-operation boundary is the canonical producer for the cross-custody physical no-late fact, while the file-backed/store ownership owner solely allocates and revalidates the opaque exact claim and stable-resource/alias binding. No separate recovery-history producer, historical summary, ledger, or startup-specific producer is a prerequisite. Recovery and operation history can preserve identity, disposition, and uncertainty but cannot substitute for a store observation proving that every covered route/effect within the selected profile's declared physical reachability universe that can reach the stable backing resource has completed or ceased. The observation does not establish persistence of already-visible data and makes no claim about routes or actors explicitly outside that universe. The file-backed owner’s current canonical all-partial-lease release remains its owner fact. For an accepted complete set that startup later abandons, this packet consumes the separately proposed exact `FileBackedReleaseObservation` without redefining its per-claim ownership dispositions: only `released-all` establishes absence of live ownership for that old set; `residual` or `uncertain` remains reconciliation-required. Conversely, current writer-claim acquisition prevents a concurrent current writer but cannot alone prove that covered effects issued through predecessor or unattributed routes have ceased.

Repository evidence warrants a bounded recovery-owned protection-epoch model only if the external lifecycle target, separate coded-range-authority and recovery `CLEAN` prerequisites, post-acquisition file-backed release target, and durable session/epoch transition remain absent after review. This head does not add that model because it is not permitted to edit models and an evidence-only profile must not become a semantic owner. No executable-proof claim is made; the model/evidence gate remains explicit in `design.md` and `tasks.md`.

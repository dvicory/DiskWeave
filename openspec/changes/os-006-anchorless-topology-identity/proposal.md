## Why

DiskWeave cannot safely assemble or replace members from paths, probe order, or a single duplicated UUID. The handoff requires stable logical topology plus deterministic multi-observation identity evidence before degraded reads, rebuilds, or a macOS bridge can be trusted.

## What Changes

- Add portable stable array, slot, role, coding-position, assignment-instance, assignment-generation, and topology-epoch semantics.
- Validate immutable topology snapshots without anchoring identity to data-member metadata or physical enumeration.
- Add deterministic identity-evidence assessment for confident matches, explainable changes, clone ambiguity, insufficient evidence, conflicting assignments, and new devices.
- Define conservative writable-assembly decisions and read-only forensic candidate reporting.
- Add fixtures for cloned UUIDs, missing serial/WWN through a bridge, file identity, capacity/geometry changes, replacement, and ambiguous candidates.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `anchorless-topology-identity`: Stable logical topology and evidence-based member identity assessment.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Extends `dwv-core` with topology identities and immutable assignment snapshots.
- Extends `dwv-store` with richer portable identity observations and deterministic assessment.
- Adds no filesystem, OS, database, runtime, or frontend type to portable topology semantics.
- Unlocks the parity-envelope comparison, explicit transaction, metadata-loss, and macOS APFS acceptance changes.

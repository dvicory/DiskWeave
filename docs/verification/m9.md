## Claim boundary

This record supports the bounded operator-controlled recovery path implemented by the current production `dwv` commands. It covers ordinary file-backed semantic behavior, SQLite adapter inspection/reopen behavior within its current evaluation claim, and one ARM64 Linux file-backed ublk publication run. It does not establish raw-device deployment, physical power-loss durability, production SQLite durability, broader frontend profiles, degraded read service, automatic repair, or P/Q recovery.

## Conservative boundaries

- Missing, corrupt/unreadable, unsupported, migration-required, and reconciliation-required recovery states remain distinct and do not initialize or migrate through observation.
- Recovery preview is non-mutating. Apply reruns current identity, topology, recovery, and exhaustive verification checks and refuses stale proposals before mutation.
- Lost or corrupt commit acknowledgement authorizes no dependent mutation until reopen establishes exact prior or exact proposed durable semantic state; any other result remains reconciliation-required.
- Fresh metadata-loss recovery carries a mandatory newly calculated checksum-baseline obligation. Partial or invalid coverage remains blocked after reopen; only complete current persisted coverage satisfies the admission prerequisite.
- Valid topologies outside the one-data/one-parity Linux publication profile are unsupported, not intrinsically invalid.
- Newly calculated checksums establish current coverage only; they do not prove historical correctness.
